# Governance

`.sarib` uses a **lead-maintainer** model (sometimes called BDFL — "benevolent
dictator for life"). This keeps the project open to contribution while direction
stays with its originator.

## Roles

- **Lead Maintainer / Project Lead — Syed Sarib Sultan ([@SyedSaribSultan](https://github.com/SyedSaribSultan)).**
  Originator and owner of the project. Final decision on the specification, the
  roadmap, releases, and what gets merged.
- **Maintainers — none yet.** The Lead may invite trusted long-term contributors
  to become maintainers (with review/merge rights). The Lead retains final say and
  can add or remove maintainers.
- **Contributors — anyone.** You contribute via pull request; see `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

## How decisions are made

- **Code & docs:** discussed in issues / pull requests; the Lead Maintainer (or a
  delegated maintainer) approves and merges. Direct pushes to `main` are disabled.
- **Specification changes:** proposed as an issue with rationale **and a reversal
  condition** (the project's decision-log convention, see `decisions/`), then
  ratified by the Lead. Ratified decisions are recorded as `D-###`.
- **Forks:** anyone may fork under the license, but the **canonical** `.sarib`
  standard is what *this* repository publishes.
