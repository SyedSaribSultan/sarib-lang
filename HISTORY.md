# .sarib

**Designing an AI-native knowledge representation language.**

A long-term research project to design a plain-text language and file format (`.sarib`) that serves as a canonical source of truth for knowledge — readable and writable by humans, queryable and editable by AI agents, and projectable into documents, outlines, graphs, boards, timelines, and context windows without duplication.

This repo is the project's working memory. Every design decision, critique, and piece of evidence lives here.

## How to navigate

| Path | What it is |
